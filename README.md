## 01 · The Quant

I'm a Mathematics (Hons.) student at the University of Delhi, interested in the intersection of **mathematical modeling, statistical research, and algorithmic trading**.

My focus is on understanding how markets behave, developing hypotheses about price formation and trading strategies, and testing those hypotheses through simulation, data analysis, and code.

I approach quantitative finance from the mathematics first. Code is the instrument; the research question comes first.

```text
Market Data → Mathematical Hypothesis → Statistical Model
     ↑                                      ↓
Live Simulation ← Backtesting ← Risk & Performance Analysis
```

> *An attractive equity curve is a hypothesis, not a proof.*
>
> — the market doesn't care about your backtest.

**Core research interests**

| Domain | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Quantitative Research** | Alpha research, statistical inference, hypothesis testing, strategy validation, out-of-sample evaluation |
| **Quantitative Trading** | Market making, execution logic, inventory management, trading simulations, risk-aware decision-making |
| **Mathematical Finance** | Probability, stochastic processes, optimization, time-series analysis, mathematical modeling |
| **Market Microstructure** | Limit order books, price-time priority, order flow, liquidity, spread dynamics, adverse selection |
| **Research Engineering** | C++ trading systems, Python research pipelines, reproducible experiments, benchmarking, visualization |

## 04 · Selected Research Projects

### `01` — LOB Terminal

**C++20 Limit Order Book & Matching Engine**

A low-latency matching engine exploring the mechanics behind electronic markets.

- Price-time priority and FIFO order queues.
- Partial fills, full fills, cancellations, and resting-order execution.
- Indexed order lookup and aggregated quantities at each price level.
- Benchmarking submission throughput and latency distributions.
- Python dashboard for inspecting order-book behavior and performance.

**Selected benchmark:** approximately 18.3 million submissions/second in one recorded benchmark run, with measured p99 latency around 305 ns. Results depend on the workload, benchmark configuration, and hardware.

<a href="https://github.com/Qivoxe"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Source_Code-GitHub-0a0a0e?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white"/></a>

### `02` — Avellaneda–Stoikov Market Making

**Inventory-Aware Quoting & Optimal Market-Making Research**

An implementation and research project inspired by the Avellaneda–Stoikov framework.

- Model reservation prices and inventory-dependent quote placement.
- Explore the relationship between spread capture and inventory risk.
- Simulate market-making decisions under changing market conditions.
- Evaluate inventory exposure, P&L behavior, and quoting parameters.

**Research question:** How should a market maker adjust quotes as inventory risk and market conditions change?

### `03` — Quantitative Strategy Research

**Statistical Modeling & Empirical Validation**

Exploring systematic ways to move from an intuitive trading hypothesis to a measurable experiment.

- Data preparation and exploratory analysis.
- Feature construction and statistical modeling.
- Train/test separation and out-of-sample evaluation.
- Transaction-cost awareness and risk-adjusted performance measurement.

*Research principle: a strategy must survive realistic assumptions, not just look good on historical data.*
